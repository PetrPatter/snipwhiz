Snipwhiz — visual pass

Proposed treatment for the two screens people actually spend time in. Every colour below already exists in LibraryWindow.xaml or in the new icon, and nothing here needs a control WPF does not have.

01 — Library

The grid currently fits as many 252-pixel tiles as will go and leaves the remainder as dead gutter down the right edge. Tiles that divide the width remove it.

1

Tiles divide the width. A fixed 252 leaves whatever does not divide evenly as permanent dead space that changes size as you resize. Tiles that stretch fill it, and the column count still adapts.

2

Search reads as a search field. Today it is an empty rounded rectangle with no icon and no placeholder, which reads as a broken control. The capture count moves up beside it, out of the footer.

3

Copy and Delete on hover, over the thumbnail. This is where the preview screen's Copy button goes. Re-copying an old capture becomes one hover and one click instead of opening a whole screen.

4

The app draws its own title bar. Stock Windows chrome saying "Snipwhiz Library" sits above a designed dark surface and fights it. Spec 1 called for frameless and it never happened.

5

Thumbnails get a light mat. Your captures are mostly dark terminals on a near-black tile, so the picture and the chrome bleed together. A light surround gives every capture an edge whatever is in it.

02 — Editor

The canvas and the capture are currently near-identical shades of black, so the edge of the image is invisible. Everything else here follows from giving the picture somewhere to sit.

6

The rail hugs its tools. It currently stretches the full window height with fourteen icons floating in it. Grouping them by family — shapes, marks, effects, crop — and letting the container end gives the canvas the room back.

7

Copy lives here, and it has to. Copy exists today only inside the preview screen. The editor has none, and Key.C is Crop. Removing the preview without this leaves no way to get a stored capture onto the clipboard at all.

8

The capture sits on a surface that is not the capture. A checkered mat and a soft shadow, so a dark screenshot has a visible edge. Right now the canvas and a dark capture are the same black and the picture has no boundary.

9

The style pill follows the selection. Colour, size and delete, floating near the object rather than in a permanent panel. It exists already — this is mostly about giving it somewhere legible to sit.

One thing your request changes that is worth deciding on purpose. Going straight from the library to the editor deletes PreviewView — a whole screen, which is a good deletion. But it also deletes the only Copy button in the app. The proposal above puts Copy in two places: on the tile for a quick re-copy without opening anything, and in the editor for after you have annotated. That is fewer clicks than today for both jobs.
